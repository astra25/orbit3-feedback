# Orbit Three feedback

Orbit Three is a small strategy game: place a mark, the outer ring turns one step, then lines are scored.

Play it at **https://www.orbit3.app**

This repository is only for feedback. It holds no game code.

## Send feedback

[Open an issue](https://github.com/astra25/orbit3-feedback/issues/new/choose) and pick one of:

- **Something went wrong**: a glitch, a wrong result, a layout problem.
- **Feedback or idea**: what was confusing, what was fun, what you would change.

It helps a lot if you mention:

- your device and browser (for example "Pixel 8, Chrome" or "iPhone 15, Safari");
- the version shown at the bottom of Settings in the game;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened.

A screenshot is welcome. Please don't include personal information.

## Privacy

The game stores your scores and settings on your device only. The privacy policy is at https://www.orbit3.app/privacy.html. Privacy questions can be asked here as an issue.
